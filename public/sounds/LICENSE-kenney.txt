Sound effects in this folder are re-encoded (mono MP3, some trimmed) from three Kenney packs:
drop.mp3 = Interface Sounds drop_002, click.mp3 = Interface Sounds switch_002, zap.mp3 = Sci-fi Sounds laserSmall_001,
pop.mp3 = Interface Sounds pluck_002, whoosh.mp3 = Sci-fi Sounds thrusterFire_000, bloop.mp3 = Sci-fi Sounds slime_000,
bonk.mp3 = Impact Sounds impactPlank_medium_000, clunk.mp3 = Impact Sounds impactMetal_heavy_000, tada.mp3 = Interface Sounds confirmation_002.
